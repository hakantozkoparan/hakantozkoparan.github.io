# FitUp Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

FitUp operates the FitUp app. You can contact us about privacy through **Profile → Help** in the app.

## Information we process

- **Workout data on your device:** Your goal, fitness level, equipment, selected training days, programs you create, workout history, and app preferences are stored in encrypted form on your device. FitUp does not offer accounts or sync across devices, and we do not upload your workout history to our servers as an account record.
- **Support requests:** If you submit the Help form, we receive the name, email address, message, app language, and app version you provide. We use these details to review and respond to your request. Support messages are stored on a server accessible to authorized FitUp staff.
- **App usage and analytics:** Firebase/Google Analytics collects events about app launches, screens, and selected interactions. FitUp sends limited event parameters such as a content ID, program day, or workout duration. We do not add your name, email address, custom workout names, or search terms to these events. Firebase may also process an app instance identifier, device identifiers where available, and technical device information as part of its service.
- **Purchases:** On iOS, FitUp Pro subscriptions are purchased through the App Store. RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription information to verify your entitlement and unlock Pro access. FitUp does not receive your full payment card details.
- **Technical requests:** When you connect to our servers to load content or images or to submit a support request, your IP address and request-related technical information may be processed in network and security logs.

## Reminders and advertising measurement

If you choose training days and grant notification permission, your device schedules a reminder titled **FitUp** for 9:00 a.m. local time on those days. You can turn off notifications in your device settings. These reminders do not use a remote push notification service.

FitUp does not display third-party ads in the app. We may advertise FitUp through Google Ads. Google Analytics/Firebase is linked to Google Ads, so app events may be used in Google's products to measure campaign performance. Personalized advertising is disabled for this link. FitUp does not request permission to access the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) and keeps ad personalization signals disabled.

## Why we use information and who receives it

We use information to operate the app, provide workout content, verify Pro access, answer support requests, protect the service, and measure and improve app usage. Apple, RevenueCat, Google Firebase/Analytics, and our hosting providers may process information to provide these services. We do not sell your data.

## Retention and your choices

You can delete workout history and preferences stored on your device through **Profile → Reset local data**. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data; records that were not backed up cannot be recovered. Analytics records and support messages are retained according to the providers' retention settings and operational needs. To request access to or deletion of a support message, use **Profile → Help**. You may also use that form to exercise applicable rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information.

## Security and children

We use HTTPS for data in transit, encrypt local app records, and restrict access to support messages to authorized staff. No security method is completely effective. FitUp is not designed for children.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the current version on the page linked from the app.
